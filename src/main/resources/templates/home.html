About locations & privacy

Supports Going to Work (24 → 93) and Going Home (93 → 24), with separately checked stop order for each direction. Nearby-stop search covers 2 km around a confirmed point. A street result only helps you position the map; you confirm the entrance yourself. Numbered addresses are sent to the U.S. Census geocoder when you search. Place names, and fallback searches after an unmatched address, go to Photon. Census positions are estimated along a street and require you to confirm the entrance. Walking coordinates are sent to Valhalla when you choose a nearby stop. Map links open Google Maps. Saving is optional.
