Jev can compare departures that fit your limits, or review service advisories and your trip update. Start a comparison from your departure results. The planner calculates the times and enforces your limits.

Evaluating sends selected public stop names, routes, trip timing context, up to three current advisories and this optional message to TypeSafe AI. Home/work addresses, map coordinates and browser credentials are excluded. Avoid putting personal information in your message.

Jev is not enabled on this deployment yet. All regular planning and trip features remain available.

Jev's probabilities describe its judgments. They are not your chance of catching a bus. No route change or boarding confirmation happens automatically.
